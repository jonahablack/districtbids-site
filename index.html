Know which public service contracts are coming up, and who to talk to.

District Bids is a small business that tracks service contracts held by districts, cities, and schools: who has each contract, what it pays, when it renews, who bid on it, and who manages it. It's built for small contractors who want a fair shot at that work.

How it works

  1. 1

    We read the public records

    Board meeting agenda packages, minutes, and other public documents from districts and agencies.

  2. 2

    We pull out the contract details

    Current contractor, price, term and renewal date, who bid, and the district manager.

  3. 3

    You see what's coming up

    Know which contracts are nearing renewal and who to contact before the next bid.

Who it's for

Small contractors who do service work for public buyers, such as:

  • Landscaping & grounds
  • Snow removal
  • Cleaning
  • Pressure washing
  • Pool service
  • Fencing
  • Similar services

We cover public buyers like Florida community development districts, Colorado metro districts, cities, school districts, and similar agencies.
