I keep track of which companies have the contracts with local districts, cities and schools, for any kind of work.

For each job I write down who has it, what they get paid, when the contract is up, who else bid on it, and who manages the district. I get it all from the meeting documents these places post for the public.

Why it helps

  • You can see which jobs in your line of work are coming up soon.
  • You know what the company doing it now gets paid.
  • You know who you'd be bidding against.
  • You know who to call before the job goes out to bid.
