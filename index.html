I track government contracts across local districts, cities, and schools so you can see which jobs are coming up, what your competitors charge, and who to contact before an RFP goes live.

What I track for every contract

For every local deal, I dig through public meeting records to pull the exact details you need.

  • Incumbent vendor. Who holds the contract right now.
  • Pricing and value. What the current provider is getting paid.
  • Expiration dates. Exactly when the contract ends or comes up for renewal.
  • Competitor intel. Who else bid on the job last time.
  • Decision makers. Who manages the district or procurement for that contract.

Why contractors use District Bids

  • Spot opportunities early. Get a head start on local work before it's listed on public bid portals.
  • Price accurately. Know what the incumbent charged so you can bid competitively.
  • Know your competition. See who bid on past cycles and who you'll likely face.
  • Build relationships first. Know exactly who to call long before the project goes out to bid.
